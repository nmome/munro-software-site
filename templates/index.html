Lead Catch: answer every form and missed call on your own.

A ready-made workflow that replies to web form and missed-call leads with Quo, Gmail and Google Sheets. You import it, connect your accounts, and it runs in your own n8n.

Email us for availability

What it does

  • Every web form lead gets an instant email reply. They hear back right away that you got their message.
  • Every missed caller gets a text back. It goes out from your Quo number, and the call is logged.
  • Every lead lands in one Google Sheet. Form leads and missed calls sit in the same sheet, each with its status.
  • One follow-up three days later. Only to leads who have not replied. By email, or by text through Quo when there is no email.
  • Replies stop the chase. When a lead texts back, they are marked Replied and never get the follow-up.

Texting guards built in

Every text names your business and ends with a STOP line. Only one follow-up goes out, never after 14 days, and only at 11:00 local time. A reply asking to stop sets a do_not_text flag. Phone-only form leads are texted only if your form shows a consent line, and the wording is included.

What you need

Lead Catch runs on your own accounts. Before you buy, check you have:

  • n8n, Cloud or self-hosted. A Make version is coming later.
  • A Quo account where you are the workspace Owner or an Admin, so you can create an API key and webhooks
  • Carrier registration approved in Quo before the texts are turned on
  • A Google account, for Gmail and the Leads sheet
  • A website form that can post to a webhook URL
  • A mobile phone you can call and text from, for the tests

Those tools bill you directly and may have their own costs, like a phone plan that can send texts. Lead Catch does not include them.

Workflow template · n8n

Lead Catch Follow-ups: win the jobs you already quoted.

Three n8n workflows that run on the same Leads sheet as Lead Catch, so there is no second spreadsheet to keep. Each one does a single job.

  • Estimate chase. Once a day it checks every open estimate and sends one nudge on day 2, day 7 and day 21. Email when there is one, a text only on the early touches and never after 14 days.
  • Review requests. Type the date a job was done and the customer gets one review request email the next day, then one reminder 3 days later unless you mark the job Reviewed in the sheet first.
  • Past-customer campaign. Paste in your customer list once a year. During the window you pick, like a spring tune-up season, it emails a small batch each day so the whole list goes out over a couple of weeks.

What you need

  • n8n, Cloud or self-hosted
  • A Google account, for Gmail and the Leads sheet
  • A Quo account, only if you want the estimate chase to text. Texts stay off until carrier registration is approved.
  • The Lead Catch Leads sheet, or a sheet set up the same way
  • A Google Business Profile, for your review link
