Clips privacy policy

What the clip posting app does with your data.

This policy covers the clip posting app run by Munro Software Company LLC in Arizona, USA. The app lets you sign in with Google or TikTok and post short videos to your own YouTube channel or TikTok account. It does not cover the rest of munrosoftwarecompany.com, which has its own privacy policy. Use of the app is also covered by our terms of service.

Effective date: November 3, 2026.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to your channel. When you connect YouTube, Google's own privacy rules also apply. You can read them in the Google Privacy Policy.

When you connect your Google account, the app accesses and stores:

  • An OAuth access token and refresh token, so the app can upload on your behalf.
  • Your channel ID and channel name, so you can see which channel a video will go to.
  • The video ID, title, description and privacy setting of each video the app uploads for you, so we can show you its status.

The app only asks for permission to upload videos. It does not read your watch history, subscriptions, comments, email or contacts, and it does not change or delete videos it did not upload.

TikTok

When you connect your TikTok account, the app accesses and stores:

  • An OAuth access token and refresh token, so the app can post on your behalf.
  • Your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar, so you can see which account a video will go to.
  • The publish ID and status of each video the app sends for you, plus the caption and privacy setting you chose.

The app asks TikTok for the video.upload permission, which sends a video to your TikTok inbox as a draft for you to review, and the video.publish permission, which posts a video straight to your profile when you choose that. It also uses user.info.basic to show your account name. It does not read your followers, messages, likes, watch history or other videos.

How we use it

We use this data only to post the videos you ask us to post and to show you whether each post worked. We never sell it, never use it for advertising, and never use it to train AI models. The video files themselves are deleted from our storage once the upload is finished.

Who we share it with

  • Google, when you post to YouTube. The video and its details go to YouTube through YouTube API Services.
  • TikTok, when you post to TikTok. The video and its details go to TikTok through its Content Posting API.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts this site and handles basic technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages.

We may also share information if the law requires it. We share it with no one else.

Cookies

The app does not place, read or store cookies or similar technologies on your device or browser, and we do not use advertising or analytics cookies. We do not track you across other websites.

Disconnecting and deleting your data

You can remove the app's access at any time. For Google, go to your Google security settings and remove the app. For TikTok, open the TikTok app, go to Settings and privacy, then Security, then Apps and services, and remove it.

You can also ask us to delete everything we hold about you by emailing munrosoftwarecompany@gmail.com. We delete your tokens and stored data within 7 days of your request or of you revoking access. Videos already posted stay on YouTube or TikTok until you delete them there.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to munrosoftwarecompany@gmail.com. Munro Software Company LLC, Arizona, USA.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date at the top. If a change affects how we use data you already gave us, we will tell you before it takes effect.
