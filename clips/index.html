Munro Clips

Post short videos to your own accounts

Munro Clips is a clip posting app from Munro Software Company LLC. You sign in with Google or TikTok, pick a finished short video, and the app posts it to your own YouTube channel or TikTok account when you ask it to.

Munro Clips is not open to the public yet. It still has to pass YouTube and TikTok review.

What it can do

  • Upload a video you choose, with the caption and privacy setting you choose.
  • Show whether the post went through.

What it cannot do

Read your watch history, subscriptions, comments, email, contacts, followers, messages or other videos.

You need the rights to every video you post.
