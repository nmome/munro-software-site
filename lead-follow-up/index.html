The problem

You can't answer the phone with your hands in a water heater. Someone fills in your website form at 9pm and hears nothing until morning. In the meantime they call the next business on Google.

Most of those leads aren't lost because you're slow. They're lost because nobody told them you got the message. A quick "we got it, we'll be in touch" is often all it takes to keep them from calling someone else.

What it does

Four things, set up once:

  • Every web form gets an instant reply. The moment someone sends your form, they hear back that you got it and will be in touch.
  • Every missed call gets an instant text. It goes out from your own business number, through your own phone system. After hours it says you're closed and will call back.
  • Every lead gets one follow-up three days later. Only if they haven't heard from you or replied yet. By email when we have one, by text for Arizona numbers.
  • Every lead lands in one Google Sheet. It sits in your account, so you can see each lead and whether it's been answered.

It runs on your accounts, in your name. We don't hold your customers' details, and we don't bill you for software.

Questions

Do I have to change my phone number or my website?

No. It uses the number and the website form you already have. The only change to your site is connecting the form, and adding a short consent line under the phone field.

What if my phone system can't send a text to a missed call?

We check that on the call, before you pay anything. If your system can't do it on your number, we tell you, and we talk about what would.

Is it OK to text my leads?

It only texts people who just called you or contacted you. Every text names your business and says "Reply STOP to opt out", none carries an offer, there's only ever one follow-up, and anyone who asks to stop is never texted again. Nothing goes out until your number passes carrier registration. That's how the build is set up, not legal advice, so ask your own lawyer if you have a specific worry.

Who owns it?

You do. Every account is in your name and billed to you. We get a login while we build, and you remove it at handover.

How long does it take?

It depends on your setup, and the written quote names the timeline. Carrier registration for texting usually takes a day or two and can take up to three weeks. Email replies can go live before that.

What happens when a lead replies?

They're talking to you, the same as any text or email. Their reply shows up in your phone or inbox, the sheet marks them as replied, and they won't get the follow-up.

What if something breaks?

If what we hand over does not do what the quote described, we fix it at no charge. Optional monthly upkeep keeps it running and fixes it when one of your tools changes. The full refund and terms wording is on the homepage.
