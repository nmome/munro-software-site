Your coach thinks on your phone. Your data syncs only if you ask.

FitnessAI generates your workouts and nutrition guidance on your iPhone, so coaching conversations never leave the device. Your training log backs up to your account only when you turn backup on.

What it does

  • A coach that runs on your phone. Workout and nutrition guidance is generated on device using Apple Intelligence. What you tell the coach is not sent to us, and not sent to any model provider.
  • Food logging that knows the numbers. Search by name or scan a barcode against USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts. Only your search text or barcode is sent, never your health data and never anything identifying you.
  • Progress you can read at a glance. Weight, training volume, and consistency tracked over time, and written back to Apple Health so the rest of your iPhone stays in sync.
  • Share with a trainer, if you want one. Link a trainer and they see the log you choose to share. Nothing is shared until you link one, and you can unlink at any time.

FitnessAI requires an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, which means iPhone 15 Pro or later. That is a consequence of running the coaching model on device rather than on a server.

Common questions

How do I delete my account and my data?

Open Settings in the app, tap Account, then tap Delete Account. This removes your account and the data associated with it from our servers within 30 days. It cannot be undone.

If you cannot get into the app, email munrosoftwarecompany@gmail.com from the address on the account and we will delete it for you.

How do I change what the app reads from Apple Health?

Open the Health app on your iPhone, tap your profile picture, tap Apps and Services, then tap FitnessAI. You can turn any individual data type on or off there, at any time.

Turning a data type off stops new data from being shared. It does not delete data the app has already read. To remove that, delete your account or email us.

My data is not syncing, or it looks wrong.

First check that Health permissions are still granted, using the steps above. Apple Health permissions can reset after an iOS update or after reinstalling an app.

If permissions look right and it is still wrong, email us with a screenshot and the date range that looks off.

Which iPhones does FitnessAI support?

iPhone 15 Pro and later, because the coaching model runs on device and requires Apple Intelligence. On older iPhones the app cannot generate coaching at all, which is why we do not offer it there rather than shipping a degraded version.

I forgot my password.

Tap Forgot password on the sign-in screen and we will send a reset link to your email. If it does not arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder before emailing us. If you signed up with Sign in with Apple, there is no password to reset, so use that button instead.

How do I stop sharing with my trainer?

Open Settings in the app, tap Trainer, then tap Unlink. Your trainer loses access immediately, and no new data is shared with them after that.

How do I request a feature?

Email it. Small operation, short list, and requests from people who are actually using the app carry the most weight.

Privacy policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

The short version: your coaching conversations are generated on your iPhone and are never transmitted. Your account and, if you turn backup on, your training log are stored with Firebase so you can restore them on a new device and share them with a trainer if you choose to link one. We do not sell your data, and we never use health data for advertising or marketing.

What we collect

Account information. Your email address, and a password that is stored only in hashed form. This is what lets you sign in and keeps your data attached to you across devices.

Health and fitness data. With your explicit permission, FitnessAI reads workout and activity data from Apple Health, and writes workouts you record back to it. You choose which data types to allow, and you can change that at any time in the Health app.

If you turn on backup, data derived from Apple Health is also stored in your account with Firebase, so that you can restore your training log on a new device and share it with a trainer you have linked. We ask for that separately from the Health permission itself, and we use it only to help you manage your own training. Health data is never stored in iCloud, and it is never sold or used for advertising.

Diagnostics and usage. If you opt in, anonymized usage events are collected through TelemetryDeck, and crash and performance reports through Apple's MetricKit. Neither carries a personal identifier, neither is linked to your health data, and both exist only to find bugs and decide what to build next. Opting out changes nothing about how the app works.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information to anyone, ever.
  • We do not use your health data for advertising or marketing.
  • We do not send your coaching conversations off your device.
  • We do not send health data to our analytics providers.
  • We do not store health data in iCloud.
  • We do not share your health data with anyone other than the trainer you choose to link, and the hosting provider that stores your backup on our behalf.
  • We do not use your health data for anything other than the features you asked for.

AI features

FitnessAI's coaching runs on your iPhone, using the on-device model that Apple ships as part of Apple Intelligence. Your messages, your training log, and your health data are processed locally on the device. There is no cloud model in the loop. Nothing you say to the coach is sent to us, sent to a model provider, retained on a server, or used to train any model.

This is also why FitnessAI requires iPhone 15 Pro or later. The model runs on your hardware, so the app needs hardware that can run it.

Service providers

We use a small number of third-party services to run the app. Each processes data only on our instructions, and only for the purposes described here.

  • Firebase Authentication. Sign-in, using either email and password or Sign in with Apple.
  • Cloud Firestore. Account backup and restore, and trainer sharing when you have linked a trainer.
  • TelemetryDeck and Apple MetricKit. Opt-in usage analytics, and crash and performance diagnostics. No health data is sent to either.
  • USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts. Food lookups. Only your search text or scanned barcode is sent. No personal or health data is included in those requests, and neither service is told who you are.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your data is removed from our systems within 30 days, and backups are purged on a rolling basis after that. Anonymized diagnostic data that cannot be linked back to you may be retained.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is limited to what is necessary to operate the app. No system is perfect, and we will not pretend otherwise, but health data is treated as the most sensitive thing we hold.

Children

FitnessAI is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email munrosoftwarecompany@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights under the GDPR or the CCPA, and we honor those requests regardless of where you live.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top of this section, and for significant changes we will notify you in the app.
