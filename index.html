What we build

Right now, one thing: a lead-source attribution dashboard that connects your ad platforms (Google Ads, Meta, LSA) to your signed-case data and shows cost-per-signed-case by channel. Most firms can see calls by source through CallRail or WhatConverts. Almost none can see signed cases by source. That's the gap.

Beyond the dashboard, we build focused internal tools and integrations for small firms. Intake-to-CRM glue, custom reports, automations that sit on top of the case management software you already use. Not a platform. Not a rebuild. A narrow tool that slots in.

Who it's for

Small personal injury, mass tort, and family law firms with 2 to 15 attorneys, running paid lead generation and trying to spend smarter on it. Based in the Phoenix metro, working with firms here first.
