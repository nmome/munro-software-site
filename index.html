How it works

From first email to a working setup

Three steps, and you see the price before anything starts.

  1. Tell us about your leadsSend a short email about where your leads come from and the tools you use today.
  2. Get one written quoteYou get a quote that says what we will build, the price and the timeline.
  3. We build and hand it overWe set it up on your own accounts, test it with you and hand it over working.

How payment works

Setup work is paid in two parts. Half of the quoted price is due before we start building, and the rest is due when the automation is handed over and working. Monthly upkeep is billed each month. Templates are paid once at checkout. All prices are in US dollars, and card payments are processed by Stripe.

Refunds and cancellations

Setup work

If you cancel before we start building, your deposit is refunded in full. Once the build has started, the deposit covers work already done and is not refundable. If what we hand over does not do what the quote described, we fix it at no charge, and if we cannot fix it, we refund what you paid.

Monthly upkeep

Cancel at any time by email. You are not billed for any month after you cancel. A month already paid is not refunded.

Templates

If a template does not import or does not work as described, email us within 14 days of purchase and we will help you fix it or refund it in full.

Terms

Every setup project starts with a written quote that says what we will build, the price, and the timeline. Work outside that quote is quoted separately before we do it. Automations run on your own accounts, such as your email, phone and Google tools, so you keep full ownership of them and of your data. We only use your data to build and run what you asked for, and we never sell it.

Privacy for the FitnessAI app is covered by its own privacy policy.
