Privacy policy

What happens to the information you send us.

This policy covers munrosoftwarecompany.com, run by Munro Software Company LLC in Arizona, USA. The FitnessAI app has its own privacy policy.

Effective date: October 27, 2026.

What we collect

This site has no accounts and no sign-up forms. We only get personal information when you choose to send it to us, usually by email or when you pay for something. That can include:

  • Your name and email address.
  • Anything you put in a message, such as your phone number, your business name, or details about how your business works.
  • Payment details when you buy from us. These go straight to Stripe and we never see your full card number.

We use it to answer you, quote and do the work you asked for, and keep records the law requires. We never sell it.

Who we share it with

We only share your information with the services that run the business:

  • Our email provider (Google), which holds the messages you send us and our replies.
  • Stripe, which processes payments.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts this site. Like any web host it handles basic technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver and protect the pages.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

Do Not Track and tracking by others

We do not track you across other websites, and we do not use advertising or analytics cookies. Because there is no tracking to turn off, the site works the same whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track signal. We do not allow other companies to collect information about your activity over time and across different websites through this site.

Seeing or changing your information

To see what we hold about you, correct it, or ask us to delete it, email munrosoftwarecompany@gmail.com. We will reply within two business days. We may need to keep some records, like invoices, where the law says we must.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date at the top. If a change affects how we use information you already sent us, we will also email you before it takes effect.
