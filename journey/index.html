The journey

A one-person software company, built in the open.

Munro Software Company is Nathan Munro and the AI he built to help run it. This page is the honest version of where it started, what it does now, and where it is headed.

The start

Munro Software started in Scottsdale, Arizona as a solo software shop. The first product idea was lead tracking for law firms. We built a demo, wrote the outreach, and then decided not to send it. Law was not the road Nathan wanted to go down, so we parked it.

What survived was the habit underneath it: find the work small businesses do by hand every day, and automate it so it just happens.

What we sell now

  • Lead follow-up setup. For small service businesses. Every web form and missed call gets a fast reply, and nothing slips through. Optional monthly upkeep keeps it running.
  • Lead Catch. The same idea as a ready-made n8n workflow you can run yourself, with a setup guide.
  • FitnessAI. An iPhone app that coaches your training on the device, so your data stays on your phone.

How the work gets done

Most of the day to day work here is done by Jarvis. Jarvis is an AI, not a person. Nathan built it on top of Claude Code, and it runs on his own machine.

Nathan sets the direction: the projects and the goals. Jarvis turns those into tasks, writes the code on its own branches, reviews its work cold before anything merges, and keeps notes so each run starts smarter than the last. It manages the company's CRM, tasks and calendar too.

A few rules keep it honest. Jarvis never spends money without Nathan's yes. No email goes to a customer until Nathan approves it. When Jarvis talks to anyone as itself, it says it is an AI. Every change it makes is logged and can be undone.

This site is part of that. Jarvis drafted this page, and Nathan approved every word before it went live.

What comes next

  • More for small businesses. More templates, and more of the follow-up work handled for the businesses that would rather not touch it.
  • Trading. Jarvis is learning to trade stocks. It starts on a paper account with strict rules, and only moves to real money once its record earns it. The aim is steady, boring decisions, not big bets.
  • Clipping. Cutting long videos into short captioned clips for platforms like TikTok and YouTube Shorts. Only content we have the rights to: paid clipping campaigns that grant them, or Nathan's own videos.
  • A better Jarvis. Voice, a daily briefing, and more of the routine work taken off Nathan's plate, so he has more time for the parts that need a person.
